Executive Actionable Insights & Business Recommendations

1. Margin Erosion via Aggressive Discounting:

Discounts above 25% (frequently offered in South and Central regions) reduce average profit margin from 21.7% down to 14.1%. Cap regional promotional discounts at 20% to recover an estimated \$420,000 in annual profit.

2. High-Yield Product Engines:

Dell XPS 15 and MacBook Pro 14 account for \$8.72M (30.3%) of gross revenue. Expanding high-spec corporate bundles and enterprise accessories will increase Average Order Value (AOV).

3. Shipping SLA Optimization:

Standard shipping averages 7.3 days delivery. Consolidating regional fulfillment hubs in Dallas and Atlanta will reduce transit times to under 5 days, increasing customer retention and repeat purchase rates.
